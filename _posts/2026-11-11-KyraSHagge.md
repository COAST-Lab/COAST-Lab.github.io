I am Kyra S. Hagge - the first ever postdoc with the COAST Lab (or so I have been told). Even though my background is in social science (Economics and social psychology), my work fits in well with the labs interest in outreach and participatory science. In my PhD dissertations, I have looked at how individual decisions on wastewater treatment impact nutrient pollution in the Tar-Pamlico watershed in Eastern North Carolina, and studied non-monetary benefits of education through a social capital lens. (If you are interested in learning more about me and my work, go <a href="https://sites.google.com/gmx.net/kyrahagge/home"> here <a/>).

Now working on the nascent <a href="https://www.uncw.edu/news/2025/12/uncw-receives-grant-to-advance-flooding-research"> Coastal Community Flood Resilience Initiative <a/> at the <a href="https://uncw.edu/cms/">Center for Marine Science<a/> allows me to follow my research interest in how human connections impact environmental outcomes. By bringing organizations together to identify common resources and gaps, we as researchers can assist the communities living in New Hanover County in dealing with flooding in a tangible way. 
When I am not behind a computer researching or talking to interest-holders, you can find me volunteering for various social and environmental organizations, playing with my kid at one of the many playgrounds in and around Wilmington, or catching waves or dinner between Wrightsville Beach and Bald Head Island. Originally from Switzerland, I travel to Europe frequently and enjoy hiking, mountain-biking and immersing myself in other cultures/languages.
